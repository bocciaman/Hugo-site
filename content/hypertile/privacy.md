**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

Hypertile is a window manager for Mac made by A.B. Anwar. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **Hypertile collects no data.**

## What Hypertile collects

Nothing. Hypertile has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It does not send information about you or your Mac to me or to anyone else.

## What Hypertile accesses on your Mac

- **Accessibility permission.** macOS requires this permission for an app to move and resize other apps' windows. Hypertile uses it only to arrange your windows. It reads window positions, sizes and titles on your Mac to do that, and never records or transmits them.
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** Hypertile listens only for the key combinations you've assigned to it. It does not log or record your typing.
- **Settings and saved layouts.** Your preferences, shortcuts and saved window layouts are stored in files on your Mac. They never leave your Mac unless you copy them yourself.

## Purchases

If you buy Hypertile from the Mac App Store, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I don't receive your payment details.

## Children

Hypertile doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
