faq:
  - q: "Which apps can KeyMap read?"
    a: "Keyboard Maestro, Karabiner-Elements, Hypertile and macOS's own shortcuts (Spotlight, screenshots, Mission Control and the rest) are read directly from their settings, including disabled macros and app-specific groups. For any other app, KeyMap reads the shortcuts in its menus, and you can add global hotkeys it can't see by hand."
  - q: "Does KeyMap record what I type?"
    a: "No. KeyMap never watches your keyboard. It only reads the settings other apps have saved, on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere."
  - q: "Will KeyMap change my shortcuts?"
    a: "No. It shows you what's taken, what's free and what clashes — you make any changes in the app that owns the shortcut, and KeyMap updates by itself."
  - q: "Why does KeyMap ask for Accessibility permission?"
    a: "Only to read the shortcuts shown in other apps' menus, and only if you track an app that way. Apps whose settings KeyMap reads directly don't need it."
  - q: "What's the Hyper key?"
    a: "Many people use Karabiner-Elements to turn Caps Lock into <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd> pressed together — a whole new layer of shortcuts no app uses by default. KeyMap shows Hyper as its own modifier, and Karabiner sublayers (hold Hyper, then a key) too."
  - q: "Which Macs are supported?"
    a: "Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later."
