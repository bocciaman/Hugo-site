faq:
  - q: "How do I switch to another book?"
    a: "Tap the book at the top of the main screen, or the Books button, and choose a book. Books you've already started also come up first when you search, so nothing gets added twice."
  - q: "I made a mistake in a session. Can I fix it?"
    a: "Yes. Tap the book's History, then tap the session to change its pages or date. Swipe left on a session to delete it."
  - q: "My book isn't found, or the details are wrong."
    a: "Tap *Enter Manually* to add any book yourself. You can edit every detail later with *Edit Book*, and use your own photo as the cover."
  - q: "Scanning doesn't work."
    a: "Scanning needs camera access. Turn it on in Settings › Legi › Camera. Point the camera at the barcode on the back cover, or at the ISBN number printed inside the book."
  - q: "How do I log pages from Shortcuts?"
    a: "Open the Shortcuts app, add the *Log Pages* action from Legi, and enter the page you stopped on. Leave the book empty to use your current book."
  - q: "How do I back up or move my reading log?"
    a: "Tap Books, then the ⋯ menu, then *Export as Text*. Save the file to Files, or send it anywhere you like."
  - q: "Does Legi collect any data?"
    a: "Legi has no accounts and no analytics. Your log stays on your iPhone. When you search for a book, the search is sent to Open Library to find it. See the [privacy policy](/legi/privacy/)."
  - q: "Where does the book information come from?"
    a: "From [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), a free, open catalog of books run by the Internet Archive."
