**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

Legi: Reading Log is an iPhone app made by A.B. Anwar. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your reading log stays on your iPhone, and I never receive any of it.**

## What Legi stores

Your books, reading sessions, and cover photos are stored on your iPhone only. Legi has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you log is sent to me or to anyone else.

## Book searches

To find a book, Legi sends what you type in the search box (a title or author), or the ISBN number you scan, to [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), a free book catalog run by the Internet Archive. Open Library sends back matching books, their page counts, and cover images.

That request contains only the search text or ISBN, along with the app's name and a contact address so Open Library knows which app is asking. It includes nothing that identifies you, such as an account, name, or device identifier. Like any website, Open Library can see the network address the request comes from. Its handling of requests is covered by the [Internet Archive's privacy policy](https://archive.org/about/terms.php).

Legi only contacts Open Library when you search or scan. Once you add a book, its details and cover are saved on your iPhone and are not fetched again.

## Camera and photos

- **Camera.** Legi uses the camera only while you scan a book's barcode or ISBN. The scanning happens on your iPhone. No images are saved or sent anywhere; only the ISBN number is used, for the search described above.
- **Photos.** If you choose your own cover photo, iOS lets you pick a single image. Legi saves a copy of that image with the book on your iPhone. It cannot see the rest of your photo library.

## Shortcuts and export

The Log Pages action in Shortcuts and the plain-text export only share your data where you choose to send it.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a book removes it and all its sessions. Deleting the app removes everything Legi has stored.

## Children

Legi doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
