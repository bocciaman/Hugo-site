faq:
  - q: "Does PastePost post for me?"
    a: "No. PastePost gets the post ready and copies it. You paste it into the site and press Post yourself, so nothing goes out by accident and no account needs connecting."
  - q: "Why does the second paste give me a photo?"
    a: "That's two-step paste. Most websites accept only one thing per paste, so PastePost puts the caption on the clipboard first, then each photo after you paste. Turn it off in Settings › Two-step paste, or use *Together for Notes* to copy everything at once."
  - q: "macOS asks to allow PastePost in Input Monitoring. Why?"
    a: "So two-step paste moves to the next photo right after you press ⌘V. PastePost only watches for ⌘V and never records your typing. It's optional: without it, press ⌘→ to step forward."
  - q: "Can I use posts I already have in folders?"
    a: "Yes. In Settings › Post Folders, click *Add Folder…* and pick a folder with one folder per platform (like *instagram* and *x*), each holding one folder per post with a caption.txt and the photos."
  - q: "Help Me Write is missing or greyed out."
    a: "Help Me Write needs a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. Everything else in PastePost works without it."
  - q: "Does PastePost collect any data?"
    a: "No. There's no account, no analytics and no server. See the [privacy policy](/pastepost/privacy/)."
