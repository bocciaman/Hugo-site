**Effective date:** November 25, 2026

PastePost is a Mac app made by A.B. Anwar that keeps your social media posts (captions, photos and videos) ready to copy and paste. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **PastePost collects no data.**

## What PastePost collects

Nothing. PastePost has no accounts, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Your posts, captions, photos, videos and settings stay on your Mac and are never sent to me or to anyone else.

## What PastePost accesses on your Mac

- **Files and folders you choose.** PastePost opens only the files and folders you pick, drag in or paste. Posts you make in PastePost are saved in its own folder on your Mac. It changes a file in one of your folders only when you edit a caption there.
- **The clipboard.** When you click Copy, PastePost puts your caption and photos on the clipboard. It doesn't read or keep anything else from it.
- **Input Monitoring (optional).** If you allow it, PastePost watches only for the ⌘V key, so two-step paste moves to the next photo after each paste. It never records, stores or sends your typing. You can turn it off any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring.

## Help Me Write

Help Me Write uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac. Your caption is processed on the device by Apple's model and is never sent to me.

## Purchases

PastePost is sold on the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I don't receive your payment details.

## Children

PastePost doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
