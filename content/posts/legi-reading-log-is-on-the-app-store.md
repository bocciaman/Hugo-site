I've wanted a simple way to keep track of what I read for a long time. Every reading tracker I tried asked for more than I wanted to give: an account, a social feed, a reading goal, a streak to protect. All I really wanted was to write down the page I stopped on.

So I built one. It's called **Legi**, and it's now on the App Store.

## How it works

You open Legi, pick your book, and the start page is already filled in from last time. You type the page you stopped on and tap Save. That's the whole thing.

Legi works out how many pages you read and keeps a running tally for each book: pages read, pages left, and percent finished. If you want to look back, every session is there in the book's history.

## Finding books

Search by title or author, or scan the ISBN barcode on the back of the book with your camera. Legi fills in the title, author, page count and cover from [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org). If a book isn't there, you can enter it by hand and use your own photo as the cover.

## Shortcuts and Siri

This is the part I use the most. Legi includes a **Log Pages** action in the Shortcuts app, so you can log your reading without opening the app. Put it on the Action button or your Home Screen, or just ask Siri.

## Private by design

There's no account and no sign-up. No ads, no analytics, no social feed. Your reading log stays on your iPhone, and you can export the whole thing as plain text whenever you want. The only thing that leaves your phone is a book search, which goes to Open Library to find the book. The [privacy policy](/legi/privacy/) has the details.

## Price

Legi is **$3.99, once**. No subscription, no in-app purchases. It runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
