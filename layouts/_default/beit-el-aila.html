{{/* ── About ── */}}

Beit El Aila is a private contact directory with centralized admin control. Whether you're managing a family, a club, a tribe, or any organization, admins control the roster while members stay connected — all data stored securely in a private iCloud container, never shared with third parties.

{{/* ── Features ── */}}

Features

Everything your organization needs to stay connected, organized, and private.

Private Directory

Browse and search all members with photos and contact details in one secure place.

One-Tap Contact

Call, WhatsApp, or email any member directly from their profile — one tap.

Birthdays & Anniversaries

Automatic reminders so you never miss an important date for any member.

Branches

Organize members into sub-groups or divisions to reflect your organization's structure.

Lineage & Relationships

View family trees and connections between members at a glance.

Member Edit Requests

Members suggest updates; admins review and approve before anything changes.

PDF Export

Generate a printable directory in one tap — ready to share or archive.

Bilingual

Full Arabic and English support with proper RTL layout throughout the app.

Private & Secure

Powered by CloudKit. No accounts to create, no ads, no data ever sold or shared.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026  ·  Developer: Abdallah Anwar

This privacy policy applies to the Beit El Aila — Contact Manager app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Abdallah Anwar (collectively, the "Application").

Information Collection

The Application may collect the following when you use it: your device's IP address, pages visited within the app and time spent, and your mobile operating system. Contact data entered by administrators — names, phone numbers, email addresses, and photos — is stored in your organization's private iCloud/CloudKit container and is never transmitted to any third-party server.

Cookies and Tracking

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality and analytics. Where required by law, consent will be obtained before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Third-Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services. The Service Provider may disclose information as required by law, to protect safety, or with trusted service providers bound by this policy.

Data Retention

User-provided data is retained for the duration of use plus 12 months. Automatically collected data is retained for up to 24 months. Aggregated anonymized data may be retained indefinitely.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us via the contact page. California residents have additional rights under CCPA/CPRA — contact the Service Provider to exercise them.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

The Service Provider maintains physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information. In the event of a data breach, you will be notified as required by law.

Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be posted with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Use the contact page to reach the developer.
