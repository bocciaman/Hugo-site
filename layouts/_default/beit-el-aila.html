{{/* ── About ── */}}

Beit El Aila is a private contact directory with centralized admin control. Whether you're managing a family, a club, a tribe, or any organization, admins control the roster while members stay connected — all data stored securely in a private iCloud container, never shared with third parties.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026  ·  Developer: A.B. Anwar

Overview

Beit El Aila ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All information you enter is stored exclusively in your private iCloud container via Apple's CloudKit framework and is never accessible to the developer or any third party.

Data We Store

The App stores the following information that you choose to enter:

  • Member names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Profile photos
  • Dates (birthdays, anniversaries)
  • Organizational relationships and branch assignments

This data is stored solely in your private CloudKit container. The developer has no ability to access, view, or export this data.

How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored in Apple's iCloud/CloudKit infrastructure using a private database. This means only you (and other members of your CloudKit container, as authorized by you) can access the data. The data is subject to Apple's iCloud Terms of Service and Apple's privacy practices. We encourage you to review Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how iCloud data is handled.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • The App does not send any data to the developer's servers — because there are no developer servers.
  • The App does not use any advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • The App does not track your behavior or share data with any third party.
  • The App does not require you to create an account or provide any information directly to the developer.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications (scheduled on-device) to remind you of upcoming birthdays and anniversaries. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification content is transmitted over the network or to any server.

Photos

When you add a profile photo for a member, the App uses the iOS system photo picker. This means the App does not request access to your full photo library. Only the specific photo you select is provided to the App by the iOS operating system.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has entered data into the App without appropriate consent, please contact us at the address below and we will assist you in removing it from your CloudKit container.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact the developer at bocciaman@gmail.com.
